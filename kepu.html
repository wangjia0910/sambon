叁理 · Principle

〇、三宝与「天人合一」0 · The Three Treasures and “Heaven-and-Human as One”

三宝殿所奉"三宝"，乃是天、地、人。其基本原理，是古人所说的「天人合一」——人不是孤立地活在这世上，而是与"天时""地理"时时相参：The "Three Treasures" of this hall are Heaven, Earth, and Human. Their foundational principle is what the ancients called “Heaven-and-Human as One” — a person never lives in isolation, but in constant attunement with the timing of Heaven and the patterns of Earth.

  • 天 · 八字：以你出生那一刻的年/月/日/时干支，记录你与"天时"的相参。Heaven · BaZi: the stems & branches of your birth moment record your attunement to Heaven's timing.
  • 地 · 六爻：以阴阳爻变之卦，摹写"地理"与事态之流转。Earth · LiuYao: the shifting yin–yang lines of a hexagram mirror Earth's patterns and the flow of events.
  • 人 · 神算子：以"人"这一才——来客心中的疑问——去会通天、地，给出解惑。Human · the Oracle: the human capacity — a visitor's question — meets Heaven and Earth to offer counsel.

所以本站的三殿，正是天、地、人三才：八字殿主天，六爻殿主地，神算子殿主人。三才相参，即是"合一"。Thus the three halls map to the three powers: the Hall of BaZi to Heaven, the Hall of LiuYao to Earth, the Hall of the Oracle to Human. Their counsel together is the "oneness."

一、八字是什么（用大白话）I · What Is BaZi (in plain words)

古人没有身份证号，就用"干支"给时间编了一套码。把一个人出生的年、月、日、时各配一个"天干 + 地支"，合起来正好八个字，所以叫"八字"，也叫"四柱"（年柱、月柱、日柱、时柱）。Long before ID numbers, the ancients encoded time with "stems & branches." Assign a "Heavenly Stem + Earthly Branch" to each of a person's birth year, month, day, and hour, and you get eight characters — hence "BaZi" (Eight Characters), also called the Four Pillars.

你可以把它理解成：用四个"时间坐标"，把你在天地间的出生那一刻"编码"下来。后面所有的解读，都是在对这八个字做结构分析。Think of it as four "time coordinates" that encode the exact moment you entered the world between Heaven and Earth. Every later reading is a structural analysis of those eight characters.

1. 天干地支：古人的"计数积木"1 · Stems & Branches: the ancients' counting blocks

天干（10 个，像"十位"）：甲 乙 丙 丁 戊 己 庚 辛 壬 癸。Heavenly Stems (10): 甲乙丙丁戊己庚辛壬癸.

地支（12 个，像"十二位"，也是生肖）：子(鼠) 丑(牛) 寅(虎) 卯(兔) 辰(龙) 巳(蛇) 午(马) 未(羊) 申(猴) 酉(鸡) 戌(狗) 亥(猪)。Earthly Branches (12, also the zodiac): 子(rat) 丑(ox) 寅(tiger) 卯(rabbit) 辰(dragon) 巳(snake) 午(horse) 未(goat) 申(monkey) 酉(rooster) 戌(dog) 亥(pig).

天干、地支按顺序两两搭配，60 种一循环，叫"六十甲子"。比如 2024 是"甲辰"年，2025 是"乙巳"年。本质上，这是一套比公历还古老的"日期编码系统"。Stems and branches pair in order, cycling every 60 — the sexagenary cycle. 2024 is 甲辰, 2025 is 乙巳. In essence it is an ancient date-encoding system, older than the Gregorian calendar.

2. 五行：万物的五个"属性标签"2 · The Five Phases: five labels for all things

每个天干地支都贴着一个五行标签：木、火、土、金、水，而且还分阴阳（比如甲是阳木、乙是阴木）。Every stem and branch carries a Five-Phase label: Wood, Fire, Earth, Metal, Water — each with a yin/yang flavor (e.g. 甲 is yang Wood, 乙 yin Wood).

五行之间有两条铁律，像一套"关系网"：Between the phases run two rules, like a relationship web:

  • 相生（互相滋养）：木生火 → 火生土 → 土生金 → 金生水 → 水生木（循环往复，像食物链）。Generating: Wood feeds Fire → Fire feeds Earth → Earth bears Metal → Metal carries Water → Water nourishes Wood.
  • 相克（互相制约）：木克土 → 土克水 → 水克火 → 火克金 → 金克木。Overcoming: Wood parts Earth → Earth dams Water → Water quenches Fire → Fire melts Metal → Metal chops Wood.

生活比喻：木是柴、火是焰、土是灰、金是器、水是润——古人用这套关系，去类比世间万物的生灭与平衡。A homely analogy: Wood is fuel, Fire flame, Earth ash, Metal a tool, Water moisture — the ancients used this web to analogize the rise, fall and balance of all things.

3. 十神：把"我"和别人的关系翻译成十种角色3 · The Ten Deities: translating relationships into ten roles

八字里，以日柱的天干代表"我自己"（叫"日主"或"命主"）。其余七个字，按它们和日主的五行"生、克、同、异"关系，换算成十种"角色"，统称十神：In BaZi, the Day Stem stands for "myself" (the Day Master). The other seven characters are converted, by how they generate, overcome, match or differ from the Day Master, into ten roles — the Ten Deities:

  • 比肩 / 劫财：和我同五行的（同辈、朋友、竞争者）。Rob Wealth / Friend: same phase as me (peers, friends, rivals).
  • 食神 / 伤官：我生出来的（才华、口才、子女、创意）。Eating God / Hurting Officer: what I produce (talent, expression, children, creativity).
  • 正财 / 偏财：我克制的（钱财、资源；男命也代表妻子 / 偏缘）。Direct / Indirect Wealth: what I control (money, resources; in male charts also spouse).
  • 正官 / 七杀：克制我的（规矩、职位、功名、压力；女命也代表丈夫）。Direct Officer / 7 Killings: what controls me (rules, office, status, pressure; in female charts also husband).
  • 正印 / 偏印：生助我的（学业、文书、长辈、贵人）。Direct / Indirect Seal: what nourishes me (study, documents, elders, benefactors).

看一个人八字里哪种"神"多，古人就推断他这一生会在哪些领域"戏份重"：财星旺的多和钱打交道，官杀旺的多在事业功名上打拼，印旺的多与文书学业有缘。Which deity dominates, the ancients inferred, shows where one's life has the most "screen time": strong Wealth deals with money, strong Officer/7-Killings with career and status, strong Seal with study and documents.

4. 日主强弱与"喜用神"：给命局找平衡4 · Strength & the Favorable Element: balancing the chart

八字解读的第一要务，是看日主是强是弱：生我、助我的五行多，日主就强；克我、泄我的多，就弱。The first task is to judge whether the Day Master is strong or weak: many phases that generate or support it → strong; many that control or drain it → weak.

强弱不是"好 / 坏"，而是"哪里失衡"。于是引出喜用神（对我有利的五行）和忌神（对我不利的）：Strong or weak is not "good or bad" — it is "where the balance tips." Hence the Favorable Element (helpful phases) and the Unfavorable:

  • 日主偏弱 → 喜"生扶"：补同类（比劫）和"生我"的（印）。A weak Day Master favors support: same-phase companions and the phase that generates it.
  • 日主偏强 → 喜"克泄耗"：用官杀约束、食伤泄秀、财星消耗。A strong Day Master favors draining: Officers to restrain, Output to vent, Wealth to consume.

说白了，这是古人版的"缺哪补哪"——命局哪边轻，就往哪边借力。属一种朴素的平衡观。In short, an ancient "balance what's thin": borrow strength where the chart is light — a plain philosophy of equilibrium.

5. 大运与流年：把时间切成"运势季节"5 · Fortune Cycles & Annual Luck: slicing time into seasons

八字是"出厂设置"，但人生是动态的。于是古人加上两条时间轴：BaZi is the "factory setting," but life is dynamic. So the ancients added two timelines:

  • 大运：每十年换一运，从月柱出发，阳年男 / 阴年女"顺排"、阴年男 / 阳年女"逆排"，约三日出生算一岁起运。Fortune Cycles: one per decade from the Month Pillar — forward for yang-year males & yin-year females, backward otherwise; about three days of life per year before onset.
  • 流年：每一年换一个干支，是大运之下的"当年天气"。Annual Luck: a new stem–branch each year — the "weather" beneath the decade cycle.

大运如季节、流年如当年风雨；"命好不如运好"这句老话，讲的就是命局（静态）和运势（动态）叠加后的结果。Cycles are seasons, annual luck the year's weather. "A good cycle beats a good chart" speaks to the static chart layered with dynamic fortune.

6. 一个有趣的数字：同八字的人有多少？6 · A curious number: how many share your chart?

八字四柱若完全独立，有 60⁴ ≈ 1296 万种。但月柱受年干约束、时柱受日干约束，真正合法的八字组合只有 518,400 局（60 × 12 × 60 × 12）。If the four pillars were fully independent there'd be 60⁴ ≈ 12.96 million. But month is bound by the year stem and hour by the day stem, leaving only 518,400 valid charts (60 × 12 × 60 × 12).

而全球人口已超 80 亿——也就是说，平均每个八字对应上万人，同八字而命运天差地别者比比皆是。这正是命理学绕不开的矛盾：出生时辰这套"编码"，远不足以决定一个人的实际人生。所以本站会把"同八字人数"算给你看，提醒一句：运在人为。With over 8 billion people, each chart is shared by tens of thousands, and identical charts lead to wildly different lives. This is the contradiction metaphysics can't escape: the birth-time "code" is far from enough to decide a life. So we show your "shared-chart count" as a reminder: fortune is made by the person.

二、六爻是什么（用大白话）II · What Is LiuYao (in plain words)

如果说八字是"看一生格局"，那六爻是"问一件事吉凶"。它源自《周易》的占筮，用三枚铜钱（或随机）摇出六爻，自下而上叠成一卦。If BaZi reads a whole life's structure, LiuYao asks about one matter's fortune. From the divination of the Yijing, six lines (three coins, or randomness) stack from bottom to top into a hexagram.

1. 爻与卦：阴阳积木搭成 64 卦1 · Lines & Hexagrams: yin–yang blocks build 64

每一"爻"分阴阳：阳爻（▇）、阴爻（▱）。三爻合成一个"经卦"，就是大家熟悉的八卦：乾(天) 兑(泽) 离(火) 震(雷) 巽(风) 坎(水) 艮(山) 坤(地)。两个经卦上下一叠，得"别卦"，共 64 卦（如"水火既济""地天泰"）。Each line is yin or yang: yang (▇), yin (▱). Three lines make a trigram — the familiar Eight Trigrams: 乾(Heaven) 兑(Lake) 离(Fire) 震(Thunder) 巽(Wind) 坎(Water) 艮(Mountain) 坤(Earth). Two trigrams stacked make a hexagram — 64 in all (e.g. 水火既济, 地天泰).

2. 起卦与动爻、变卦2 · Casting, moving lines & the changed hexagram

  • 起卦：随机六次得六爻，前三者为下卦、后三者为上卦，拼出本卦。Casting: six random lines; the first three form the lower trigram, the last three the upper — together the main hexagram.
  • 动爻（老阳 / 老阴）：偶有"发动"的爻，阴阳反转后，另得一"变卦"，代表事情有了转机或变化方向。Moving lines: occasionally a line "moves," flips its polarity, and yields a changed hexagram — a turning point or new direction.
  • 静卦：六爻皆"少"，无动爻，主事态暂稳。Still hexagram: no moving lines — matters are stable for now.

3. 卦辞怎么"白话"读3 · Reading the hexagram in plain words

每卦都有其意象与断语。在三宝殿里，我们不只给你卦名和古文辞，还会用白话解卦翻成大白话：先说这卦名叫什么、上面是哪个卦下面是哪个卦（各主什么意象），再讲卦辞大意，最后点出动爻意味着"事情要变"还是"暂且稳住"。这样即使没读过《易经》，也能听懂这卦在说什么。Each hexagram has imagery and a verdict. In this hall we don't just give the name and classical text — we add a plain-language reading: what the hexagram is called, which trigrams sit above and below (and what they mean), the gist of the text, and whether a moving line signals change or "hold steady." Even without reading the Yijing, you can follow it.

举例：「火水未济」——火在上、水在下，火水不相交济，卦辞讲"事未成，慎终如始、防小狐渡河湿尾"。白话就是：事情还没成，越到收尾越要小心，别在最后一步翻船。Example: 火水未济 — Fire above, Water below, the two not crossing. The text says "unfinished; be as careful at the end as at the start, lest the little fox wet its tail crossing the river." In plain words: it isn't done yet — be most careful at the finish, don't capsize on the last step.

三、它为什么"显得准"，以及该怎么看III · Why It "Seems Accurate," and How to See It

  • 巴纳姆效应：描述多为"你外表坚强内心敏感"这类人人觉得像自己的话。Barnum effect: statements like "strong outside, sensitive inside" fit almost everyone.
  • 模糊与开放：一个"财星受克"可对应破财、感情、工作变动，事后总能对上。Vagueness: "Wealth star constrained" can mean a loss, a romance, or a job change — something always fits after the fact.
  • 选择性记忆：人更记得"说中的"，自动忽略"没说中的"。Selective memory: we remember the hits and forget the misses.

八字与六爻，是古人用"阴阳五行、干支、八卦"这一套宇宙观，去推演人生的民间实践，在文化史、哲学史上自有价值。但它没有任何可证伪、可重复的科学依据。健康、财务、职业等重大决定，请交给现实信息与专业人士。BaZi and LiuYao are folk practices that use a cosmology of yin–yang, five phases, stems–branches and trigrams to reason about life; they hold real value in cultural and philosophical history. But they have no falsifiable, repeatable scientific basis. For major health, financial or career decisions, rely on real information and professionals.
