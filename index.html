何谓三宝 · 天地人What Are the Three Treasures

三宝者，天、地、人也。八字察「天之时」，六爻观「地之象」，神算子会「人之问」——三才相参，便是「天人合一」。人之生辰落地那一刻，干支节律已定，是谓「天之时」；心中一念起处，阴阳爻变成像，是谓「地之象」；以人之所问会通天地，便是这座殿宇的全部道理。The Three Treasures are Heaven, Earth, and Human. BaZi reads Heaven’s timing, LiuYao reads Earth’s signs, the Oracle meets the Human’s question — three powers in counsel, that is “Heaven-and-Human as One.” The moment you are born, the rhythm of stems and branches is set — that is Heaven’s timing. The moment a question rises in your heart, yin and yang form a hexagram — that is Earth’s sign. To bring the human question before Heaven and Earth is the whole teaching of this hall.

The hexagram is random, for counsel only; a plain-language reading follows.

      
      

    

  


  
  

    
叁人 · Human

    
神算子殿 · 神算子攀谈Hall of the Oracle · A Conversation

    

      
神算子不急于断你何事。他先观你八字、再卜一卦，略陈观感（含白话解卦），便循循善诱，与你攀谈数语，终而点出你此来心中所悬之事——此殿主人：以人心之问，会通天时与地象。The Oracle will not rush to a verdict. He reads your chart, casts a hexagram, offers his impressions (with a plain-language reading), then draws you out in conversation until he names what truly weighs on you — this hall belongs to the Human: the question of a heart meeting Heaven’s timing and Earth’s signs.

      
玩法：先完成上方「八字推演」与「六爻起卦」，再请神算子入局攀谈。解得准与不准，尽可点破——他听在耳里、记在心上，越聊越懂你。How: run the BaZi chart and cast a hexagram above, then call the Oracle in. Tell him what lands and what misses — he takes it to heart and grows attuned to you.
