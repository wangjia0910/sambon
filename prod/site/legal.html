五、算法推荐说明、关闭方式与 AI 透明度

5.1 算法用途

操作台在候选分派环节使用算法辅助排序（POST /api/tasks/:id/dispatch），依据任务属性、技能标签、半径与截止时间等特征生成候选与批次。排序结果仅为候选建议，不产生自动指派或自动裁决的法律效果。当前配置版本见操作台展示的 weight_version、rule_version 与 llm_adapter_version。

5.2 输入特征、六项权重与输出

输入：任务分类、所需技能、线上/线下与半径、截止时间、悬赏参数、账号级个性化开关状态。排序由六项权重共同决定，其取值与版本在「AI 透明度」卡片中逐项展示，并随 GET /api/config 返回。输出：候选列表 candidates 与批次 batches，以及本次模式 mode 与是否启用个性化 personalized。我们不把种族、宗教、健康、性取向等特殊类别数据用于排序；不使用大语言模型对任务审核、验收、争议裁定或账号处罚作出自动决定。

5.3 降级模式

当 algorithm_degraded 为真时，系统使用保守排序并降低个性化权重，以避免在特征不足时产生偏斜推荐；该状态会在「AI 透明度」卡片中明示。

5.4 个性化推荐的关闭方式

在「AI 透明度与配置」卡片中勾选「关闭个性化推荐」开关即可关闭账号级个性化（对应 POST /api/preferences/personalized，enabled: false）；取消勾选可重新开启。该开关服务端强制生效（enforcement: server_side），客户端无法绕过，关闭后排序仅使用与任务直接相关的非个性化特征。关闭个性化不影响任务完整性、审核与验收流程。客户端曝光/忽略回执（POST /api/tasks/:id/feedback）仅用于算法透明度说明，不改变人工闸门的结果。

5.5 AI 透明度与人工闸门

GET /api/config 返回的 ai_disclosure 以中英双语在操作台原样展示，前端不改写、不隐藏。任务审核、候选推送、结算、成果验收、自动验收、争议裁定、风控处置与账号处罚均为人工闸门：前端对管理员操作弹出二次确认并标注「人工确认」，服务端在人工确认后执行。我们不作仅依赖自动化处理、且对你产生法律效果或类似重大影响的决定；你可对人工决定通过举报通道或 privacy@sambon.net 申请人工复核。

5. Algorithmic Recommendation, Opt-Out and AI Transparency

5.1 Purpose

The console applies algorithm-assisted ranking when dispatching candidates (POST /api/tasks/:id/dispatch). It generates candidates and batches from task attributes, skill tags, radius and deadline. Ranking is advisory only: it neither auto-assigns work nor produces an adjudicated outcome. The active configuration is shown in the console as weight_version, rule_version and llm_adapter_version.

5.2 Inputs, six weights and outputs

Inputs: task category, required skills, online/offline and radius, deadline, bounty parameters and the account-level personalisation switch. Ranking is governed by six weights whose values and version are displayed item by item in the AI Transparency card and returned by GET /api/config. Outputs: the candidates list, batches, the run mode and whether personalisation was applied (personalized). Special-category data such as race, religion, health or sexual orientation is never used for ranking, and no large language model makes autonomous decisions on review, acceptance, dispute resolution or sanctions.

5.3 Degraded mode

When algorithm_degraded is true, the system falls back to conservative ranking and reduces personalisation weighting to avoid skewed recommendations under sparse features; this state is shown in the AI Transparency card.

5.4 How to disable personalisation

Tick "Disable personalised recommendations" in the AI Transparency card to turn off account-level personalisation (POST /api/preferences/personalized with enabled: false); untick to re-enable. The switch is enforced server-side (enforcement: server_side) and cannot be bypassed by the client; once disabled, ranking uses only non-personalised, task-relevant features. Disabling personalisation does not affect task integrity, review or acceptance. Client exposure/ignore receipts (POST /api/tasks/:id/feedback) serve algorithmic transparency only and never alter a human-gated outcome.

5.5 AI transparency and human gates

The ai_disclosure returned by GET /api/config is shown verbatim in Chinese and English; the front end neither rewrites nor hides it. Task review, candidate dispatch, settlement, result verification, auto-acceptance, dispute resolution, risk disposition and account sanctions are all human gates: administrator actions open a confirmation dialog labelled for explicit human confirmation, and the server acts only after that confirmation. We make no decision based solely on automated processing that produces legal or similarly significant effects; you may request human review of any decision through the reporting channel or at privacy@sambon.net.
